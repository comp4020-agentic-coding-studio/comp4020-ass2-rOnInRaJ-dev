## What this page must do
Cover what this team's studio hours do before and around the fixed field flight: rehearsing the one-question flight plan and reading the log afterwards, in the room, on the bench — distinct from the flying itself, which is already fixed content on this page. This is the page that works the prop-strike failure story.

## Where the prose goes
A new `## In the studio` section between the "This week works on `R-03`, `R-05`, `R-07`." line and "## At the field". Target ~300 words: workshop hours (~140 words, pre-flight prep and the CG-check warning) and lab hours (~160 words, reading the log after the flight).

## Must say
- Requirement IDs: R-03, R-05, R-07.
- Workshop hours: the team writes down the one question this flight will answer, and the predicted number it expects, before booking the field slot — matching the field bullet's "predict... before either test" pattern already fixed on this page, but as a studio-room activity (writing it down, on the bench) rather than restating the field predict bullet.

## The failure story

Land it in the workshop-hours half, attached to the bench CG check, as a direct warning: a CG check closed weeks ago (R-11, weeks 6 and 10) without the actual flight configuration can still be wrong, and it shows up here, on landing, not on the bench.
