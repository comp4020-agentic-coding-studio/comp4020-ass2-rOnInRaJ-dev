## What this page must do
Teach functional decomposition (one function, one owning subsystem) and the discipline of a trade study — specifically, that criteria and their weights are fixed before any option is scored. This is the only page that explains why the weights go in first.

## Where the prose goes
New prose between the `**By the end:**` line and `## Outline`. About 300 words in two paragraphs: the first works the failure story as a warning about ordering; the second explains functional decomposition, using the five subsystems named in week 1 without redefining them.

## Must say
- Decomposition allocates each function of the mission to exactly one of the five subsystems (Aero, Structures, Propulsion & Power, Avionics & Control, Operations) — name them, do not redefine what a subsystem is.
- A trade study needs its criteria weighted before any option is scored — tie this directly to the reason the outline bullet exists.
- The three configurations already named in the outline (fixed-wing, quad, VTOL) may be named again, but the new prose should explain why the ordering of weights-then-scores matters, not re-list the outline's bullets.
- If a number is needed for grounding, only the mass cap, 1800 g (`MTOW_CAP_G`), may be cited, as the one constraint every configuration is scored against regardless of choice.

## The failure story
Story 3 (the reverse-engineered trade study). Use it as the central warning: a team had already decided on fixed-wing, and the weights arrived afterwards and, remarkably, agreed with the decision already made. Land it exactly where the outline says "the trade study, and why the weights go in first" — make it the reason that bullet exists.

## Must not
- Must not describe the actual configuration trade a team runs, or the endurance and disc-loading figures from a real comparison — that is week 3's studio field objective.
- Must not discuss requirements-writing mechanics (threshold, owner, verification) — that is week 2's page.
- Must not touch aerofoils, Reynolds number, or wing sizing — week 4.
- Must not narrate the requirements and concept review assessment itself beyond naming that weights precede scores.
