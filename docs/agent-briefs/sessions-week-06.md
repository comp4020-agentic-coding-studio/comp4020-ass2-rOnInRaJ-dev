## What this page must do

Cover the four studio hours where each team sizes a tail and checks a CG range
for its own aircraft — analytical, indoor work that is distinct from the
field's generic three-position demonstration on a club trainer. No other page
does the own-design tail and CG check.

## Where the prose goes

One new section, `## In the studio`, inserted between the
"This week works on `R-02`, `R-11`." line and the `## At the field` heading.
~300 words, two paragraphs: workshop hours, then lab hours.

## Must say

- Workshop hours: each team computes a tail volume coefficient from its own
  wing (span **1600 mm**, chord **240 mm**, from `geometry`) and sizes a
  horizontal and vertical tail against it, then enters the tail into the CAD
  model started this week.
- Lab hours: the CAD model returns a mass; the team checks that mass against
  the ledger's tail-structure line (**85 g**, `massBudget.tailStructure`) and
  records the gap, then predicts where their own CG will sit and checks it
  against `R-11`'s band before the field slot.
- Name `R-02` and `R-11` explicitly, matching the spec bullets already on this
  page.
- One sentence that the field's three-position exercise is on a club trainer,
  not their own aircraft, and the point of the studio work is to have a
  predicted CG for their own design ready before that flight.
