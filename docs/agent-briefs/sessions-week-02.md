## What this page must do
Get a team's mission down to one sentence with a number in it, anchored on a real measurement (circuit time), and get R-01, R-07 and R-08 written into the ledger as constraints. This is the only page where a team commits to the length of its own mission leg.

## Where the prose goes
A new `## In the studio` section, between the existing `This week works on...` line and `## At the field`. About 300 words in two paragraphs: what the workshop hours do before the field slot (drafting the mission sentence, writing the first requirement numbers), and what the lab hours do (opening the ledger with sourced first estimates).

## Must say
- The requirement IDs this week touches — R-01, R-07, R-08 — are already stated in the file's own "This week works on" line. Do not restate that line; build past it.
- The mission sentence must name a distance and a payload (per the file's existing spec). Explain what "names a distance" rules out, using the studio's own circuit-time measurement as the source of that number, not an invented one.
- The one mass figure this page may cite is the 250 g payload (`massBudget.payload`), since `R-08` names a payload and 250 g is the only payload figure the ledger allows.
- Workshop hours: turning the field's circuit-time measurement into a mission-leg distance and writing it into the requirement sentence. Lab hours: opening the ledger file, entering first-pass estimates, and getting the lab technician to weigh a shelf part as a sourced reference figure.
