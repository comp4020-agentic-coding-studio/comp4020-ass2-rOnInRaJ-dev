## What this page must do

Cover the four studio hours where each team writes its own interface control
document and drafts a bench method for `R-13`, before the field's incident-book
walk (which maps recorded club failures onto that same document). No other
page does the ICD drafting itself.

## Where the prose goes

One new section, `## In the studio`, inserted between the
"This week works on `R-12`, `R-13`." line and the `## At the field` heading.
~300 words, two paragraphs: workshop hours, then lab hours.

## Must say

- Workshop hours: each team writes its one-page interface control document,
  naming what every subsystem hands its neighbours — mass in grams, current
  or power in watts, voltage, or a data or signal line — using their own
  design's actual handovers, not a generic template.
- Lab hours: the team writes a bench method for `R-13` (protractor and cycle
  counter, fifty cycles, one degree of repeatability — spelled in words) and,
  where a servo-to-surface interface exists on their own aircraft, bench-tests
  it in the same configuration it will fly in, not with the surface removed.
- Name `R-12` and `R-13` explicitly, matching the spec bullets already on this
  page.
- One sentence connecting the ICD drafted here to the field objective below:
  the incident-book walk maps three recorded failures onto the interfaces just
  written down, so the document has to exist before the field slot, not after.

## The failure story

Story 8 (reversed aileron servo, bench-tested wing off, found on the take-off
roll) as a full worked example inside the lab-hours paragraph — the direct
reason the bench method insists on testing an interface in its flying
configuration, not a convenient one.

## Must not

- Must not restate the lecture's interface definition or the design-freeze
  argument — apply it to their own aircraft, do not re-explain it.
- Must not describe the incident-book walk or name which failures get mapped —
  that is the fixed "At the field" block below this section.
- Must not introduce a verification matrix — week 10.
- Must not touch spar sizing, tail sizing or centre of gravity — weeks 6 and 7.

## Voice notes

- Workshop-hours paragraph reads as a document being written; lab-hours
  paragraph reads as a test being planned and then run once, in the right
  configuration.
- No suspense on the failure story — state the mistake and the fix it argues
  for in the same breath.
