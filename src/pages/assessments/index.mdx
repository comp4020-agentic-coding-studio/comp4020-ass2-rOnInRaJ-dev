Six items. Twenty marks of it are yours alone — the quizzes and your logbook —
and eighty are your team's, moderated by peer review at the end of semester.

<AssessmentsSummaryTable />

Every group item carries the same systems-engineering criterion, worth thirty
of its marks, and it asks the same four questions every time. Are the
requirements numbered and testable? Is it clear which subsystem owns each one,
and what it hands its neighbours in grams, volts, watts and data? Was a real
alternative considered, and did a number decide it? Does every requirement have
a check attached, and did you predict before you measured?

Nothing on this list rewards an aircraft that looked good on the bench.
