Two hours, Monday, for twelve weeks. The order is not the textbook order:
power comes in week 5, before structures, because the battery sets the mass and
the mass sets the wing. A team that leaves the electrical system until week 9
has already designed the wrong aeroplane.

Every lecture ends with one thing you can now do that you could not do the week
before. [Week 5](/lectures/week-05/) carries a full deck.
