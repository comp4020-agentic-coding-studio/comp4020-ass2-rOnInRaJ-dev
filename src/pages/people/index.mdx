Three of these people are university staff. Two are volunteers at Mount Sludge
Model Aircraft Club, which lends this course its field, its instructors and its
patience. The club is not paid to host you and is not obliged to keep doing it.

Course questions go to the convenor. Workshop questions go to the lab
technician, in the workshop, during a studio. Field questions go to the club, at
the field — not to course email.
