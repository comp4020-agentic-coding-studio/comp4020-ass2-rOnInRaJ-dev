Bez teaches weeks 5, 7, 8, 10 and 11, and runs the iron-bird bench rig
that every team wires their avionics onto before it goes anywhere near an
airframe.

He is the reason this course teaches power before structures. His argument,
which has never lost an appeal: the battery sets the mass, the mass sets the
wing, and a team that leaves the electrical system until week 9 has already
designed the wrong aeroplane.

Ask him about ESC timing, pack sag under load, failsafe configuration, PID
loops that will not settle, and why your receiver lost the link at 200 metres
when the range check passed on the ground.
