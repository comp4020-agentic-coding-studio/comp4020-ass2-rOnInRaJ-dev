Nadia is in the workshop for every studio. She inducts you onto the
machines, she keeps the thrust stand calibrated, and she holds the scale that
settles arguments about R-01.

She does not design your aircraft and will not tell you what to build. She will
tell you, precisely, whether the thing you have built is what your drawing said
it would be.

Ask her about hot-wire cutting foam cores, laying carbon over a spar, bonding
that will survive a hard landing, and booking the load rig before week 7 when
everybody wants it at once.
