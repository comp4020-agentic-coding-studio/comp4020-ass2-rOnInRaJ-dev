Ivo makes the go/no-go call. Wind, cloudbase, how many aircraft are
already up, whether the mower is out: his call is final and it is not a
negotiation, however far your team drove.

He keeps the pack register. Every LiPo issued to a team is logged out and
logged back in, discharged, at the end of semester — that is R-14, and it is
the only requirement on this course with no engineering content and no
flexibility whatsoever.

Ask him about the field rules, the no-fly line, what to do with a pack that has
puffed, and why the answer on a gusty day is usually no.
