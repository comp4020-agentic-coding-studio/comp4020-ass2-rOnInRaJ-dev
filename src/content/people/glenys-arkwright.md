Glenys teaches your first flights, on a club trainer, on a buddy box —
she holds a second transmitter and can take the aircraft back the instant you
lose it. Most students lose it in the first minute. That is what the buddy box
is for.

She is a volunteer. The club lends this course its field, its instructors and
its patience, and none of that is owed to you. Turn up when you said you would,
or release the slot so another team can have it.

She signs the flight line. Until your name is on it, you do not hold a
transmitter at Mount Sludge.
