Halcyon runs the ledger. If a mass appears in your design without a
line item behind it, she is the one who will ask where it came from, and she
will keep asking until somebody produces a number.

She teaches weeks 1 to 4, 6, 9 and 12, and she marks the design reviews. Her
standing advice to every team: the aircraft you can defend beats the aircraft
that looks best, every single time.

Ask her about wing sizing, low-Reynolds aerofoils, why your polar does not
look like the one in the book, and mass budgets that have quietly gone over.
