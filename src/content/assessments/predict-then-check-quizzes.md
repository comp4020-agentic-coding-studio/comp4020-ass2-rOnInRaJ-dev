## The brief

> Commit to a number before you measure it. Then find out.

Eight quizzes, in weeks 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10 and 11. Each opens on the Monday
lecture and closes before your team's field slot that week, which is the point:
the prediction has to exist before the measurement does.

The questions are short and they are about your own aircraft, not a textbook
one. What stall speed does your wing area give you? What current will your
motor pull on the flight prop? How far aft can your centre of gravity go before
`R-11` is violated?

Best six of eight count. If a week gets away from you, it costs you nothing.

## What you submit

Nothing — the quizzes are the submission, taken on the course site.

## How it is marked

Holistically, against the statement below. Show your working; that is the part
being marked.
