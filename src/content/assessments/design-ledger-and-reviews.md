## The brief

> Keep one ledger, all semester, and be able to explain every number in it on
> the day somebody asks.

Your ledger is a table of grams, watts and watt-hours with a line for every
subsystem. It opens in week 2 full of estimates and closes in week 10 full of
measurements. What is marked is everything between those two states: whether
each line is honest about what kind of number it is, and whether a change ever
happens without a reason.

The preliminary design review in week 5 baselines the power system, because
power sets battery mass and battery mass sets everything else. The critical
design review in week 9 baselines the whole aircraft. After CDR, a change needs
a written reason and a re-check of every requirement it touches.

Teams lose marks here for one thing above all others: a ledger that agrees with
itself only because somebody quietly edited the old number.

## What you submit

- the ledger, with its full revision history
- the PDR and CDR packs as presented, unedited after the fact
- the action list from each review, with what was closed and what was not
- a one-page mass and power statement at CDR, closing `R-01` and `R-09`
